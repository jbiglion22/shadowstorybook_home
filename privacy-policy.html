Privacy Policy

App: Shadow Storybook (Android) · Developer: J.Biglion · Effective date: November 12, 2026

  • We do not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.
  • There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools.
  • The app does not use the internet. Everything stays on your device.
  • Voice recordings you make are saved only on your device and are never uploaded.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, does not ask for your name, email, location, or contacts, and does not include advertising or analytics software. The app does not request internet access, so it cannot send information from your device to us or to anyone else.

2. Information stored only on your device

To work properly, the app keeps the following in its private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us.

InformationWhy
Reading progress, finished stories, solved quizzesTo continue where you left off and show progress
Settings (language, text size, read-aloud speed and voice, tilt effect)To remember your choices
Voice recordings you makeTo play your own voice when your child listens to a page

All of this is deleted when you uninstall the app. You can delete voice recordings at any time in Settings > Recorded voices.

3. Microphone and voice recordings

The app asks for microphone permission only when you tap Record to read a page aloud in your own voice. Recording happens only while the recording window is open and you have started it. Recordings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not uploaded and are excluded from Android cloud backup. You can deny the permission; the rest of the app still works.

4. Device features used

  • Motion sensor: used only to sway the light, the rope, the waves and the seaweed when you tilt the phone. The readings are not stored. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Text-to-speech: the read-aloud feature uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your device (for example, Google Speech Services). Only the story text is passed to it. The app prefers voices that work offline. If you choose a voice marked "Needs internet", that speech engine, a separate app on your device, may process the story text online under its own provider's privacy policy. The story text contains no personal information.

5. Backup

If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may back up the app's reading progress and settings to your own Google account, as it does for other apps. Voice recordings are excluded from this cloud backup. We cannot access your backup.

6. Children's privacy

Shadow Storybook is designed for children aged about 4 to 8 together with their parents. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app follows Google Play's Families Policy: it contains no ads, no in-app links to outside websites for children, and no data collection.

7. Sharing

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share it with anyone.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and change the effective date above.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact the developer.
