One light behind the screen

Just like a real shadow play, the light shines from behind the screen and only the shadows show. Children drag the light to brighten the stage and tap the shadows to make them move. Even when nobody touches them, the shadows gently breathe and sway.

Drag across the stage to move the light, then tap the tiger.

What's inside

A storybook children play with

🏮

Light & shadows

Move the light, turn it off to darken the stage, and tilt the phone to sway the light, the rope and the waves.

🐯

Tap to bring to life

Tap the tiger and it roars. Open a gourd and a goblin pops out. Little surprises are hidden on every page.

🎙️

Your own voice

Record each page in your voice, or a grandparent's. The app plays it back whenever your child wants to listen.

🌙

Bedtime mode

A dim screen, a timer, and pages that turn by themselves. The screen slowly fades out when time is up.

⭐

Guess the shadow

When the story ends, guess who made each shadow. It is there only when your child wants to play.

🎭

Shadow playground

Put the tiger, the rabbit, the swallow and other story shadows on the stage, move them around, and move the light to make your own shadow play.

Questions

Who is it for?

Children aged about 4 to 8 reading with a parent, Korean families living abroad, and teachers introducing Korean culture.

Do I need to read aloud myself?

Reading together is the heart of the app, but you can also record your voice once for each page or use your device's text-to-speech voice.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only inside the app on your device. They are not uploaded and are left out of cloud backups. You can delete them any time in Settings.

Does it need the internet?

No. Stories, pictures, and recordings are all on your device. Some text-to-speech voices from your device's speech engine may need the internet; the app shows which ones.

Which languages are supported?

English and Korean. You can switch the language inside the app at any time.
